HOW TO PLAY
🎃
1. Look at your question.
Read the question in your team's box.
🔥
2. Choose an answer.
Tap the correct answer as quickly as you can.
🪙
3. Collect gold!
A correct and fast answer gives 3–10 points.
👻
4. Keep playing.
A wrong answer gives no points. Try again with the next question.
💥
5. Finish the round.
When time is over, the Monster Egg will shake, crack, and reveal a Halloween surprise.
🏆
6. Become the Champion!
After Round 1 and Round 2, the team with the most total points wins.
